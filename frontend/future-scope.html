Future Scope & AI Architecture

Proposed technical developments for BusEase Phase II implementation.
[Note: Clearly categorized as Future Scope for college evaluation review]

FUTURE SCOPE

AI Bus Detection

Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) integration at campus gate cameras for automatic vehicle license plate & bus number recognition without manual scanning.

FUTURE SCOPE

OpenCV Passenger Counting

In-bus optical sensors using OpenCV & YOLOv8 models to count boarding passengers automatically and cross-verify with digital seat reservations.

FUTURE SCOPE

ML Bus Delay Prediction

Supervised Machine Learning regression algorithms factoring peak ECR/Highway traffic history, weather, and traffic lights to calculate hyper-accurate ETA predictions.

FUTURE SCOPE

AI Route Optimization

Dynamic Graph neural network algorithms to re-route buses during heavy traffic congestion or monsoon flooding to minimize student travel delays.
